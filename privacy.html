Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Technicade collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use technicade.tech, Technicade software products and subdomains, subscriptions, customer support, service-request forms, and related services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on which Technicade product or service you use, we may collect:

  • Account information such as name, email address, profile information, login identifiers, and authentication details.
  • Business and workspace information such as organization names, locations, members, roles, checklists, tasks, schedules, assets, reminders, reports, and operational records.
  • Content you submit, including text responses, photos, files, measurements, notes, maintenance records, service requests, and support communications.
  • Subscription and billing information such as plan, subscription status, customer and subscription identifiers, invoice status, and billing history. Full payment-card details are processed by Stripe and are not stored directly by Technicade.
  • Technical and usage information such as browser type, device information, IP-derived information, timestamps, page views, feature usage, error logs, and security events.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when requesting local or remote services, quotes, repairs, 3D printing, troubleshooting, or other Technicade work.

2. Business and employee data

Technicade products may allow businesses to create workspaces and invite employees, contractors, managers, or other users. Workspace administrators may be able to view activity, submissions, photos, checklist completion, reports, and other records created within that workspace. The business or organization that created the workspace is responsible for determining what information it collects and for providing any notices or obtaining any permissions required by applicable law or workplace policy.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide, secure, maintain, and improve Technicade products and services; authenticate users; operate subscriptions; store and display customer content; send transactional emails, reminders, alerts, reports, and account notices; provide support; troubleshoot problems; prevent fraud and abuse; process service requests; create quotes; maintain business records; and comply with legal obligations.

AI-assisted planning

The free ProjectUnstuck browser planner runs locally on your device and does not send its project inputs to OpenAI. If we accept an order for AI-assisted personalized planning, we will explain before payment what project details may be processed by OpenAI and how the plan will be reviewed and delivered. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information through ordinary service forms or project links.

4. Payments

Payments and subscription billing are processed by Stripe. Stripe may collect payment details, billing addresses, tax information, and other information necessary to process transactions. Technicade receives transaction and subscription information needed to operate paid plans but does not directly store full card numbers.

5. Service providers

Technicade uses third-party providers to operate its products and services. Depending on the product, these may include Stripe for payments and subscriptions, Supabase for authentication, databases, and file storage, Vercel for hosting and analytics, Resend for transactional email, GitHub or GitHub Pages for website hosting or source deployment, and Web3Forms for website form delivery. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

6. How information is shared

Technicade does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the service, with authorized members of the same workspace, when you direct us to share it, when reasonably necessary to protect Technicade or its users, in connection with a business transfer, or when required by law.

7. Cookies, analytics, and similar technologies

Technicade websites and software may use cookies, local storage, authentication tokens, analytics, and similar technologies to keep users signed in, remember preferences, secure accounts, understand product usage, diagnose errors, and improve performance. Some products may use privacy-focused analytics provided by the hosting platform or other service providers.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the security of Technicade products. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information, the product involved, and the status of the account or workspace.

9. Data security

We take measures intended to safeguard information, including access controls and protections offered by our infrastructure providers. Actual practices may differ by product and should be evaluated against the system in use. No online service, transmission method, or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting account credentials, using appropriate access controls, and limiting the information they upload to what is appropriate for the service.

10. Sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, Social Security numbers, financial account credentials, medical information, or other highly sensitive information unless a specific Technicade product expressly requests it and you have a lawful reason to provide it. Service-request forms and ordinary workspace fields are not intended for highly sensitive personal information.

11. Children

Technicade products and services are generally intended for adults, businesses, and workplace use and are not directed to children under 13. Do not knowingly submit a child's personal information unless you have a lawful basis to do so and the applicable product is being used in a manner permitted by law.

12. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law and your relationship to a Technicade workspace, you may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of personal information. Data controlled by a business customer may need to be handled through that organization's administrator. Requests may be sent to support@technicade.tech.

13. U.S. processing

Technicade operates from the United States and uses service providers that may process information in the United States and other locations. By using the services, you understand that information may be processed in jurisdictions different from your own.

Remote support sessions and customer consent

When remote technical support is expressly offered and agreed, an authorized technician may be able to view your screen, interact with your computer, inspect device configuration, or access files and applications made available during the session. We will identify the approved remote-access provider or tool before the session. Remote support is not activated merely by visiting this website or submitting an inquiry. You must separately authorize each attended session, and you may end the session at any time.

We may document the service requested, approximate session times, technician identity, authorization and work performed, and troubleshooting results for service quality, security, disputes and accounting. We do not ordinarily need to record your screen or session audio/video; if recording is proposed, we will disclose it and seek separate consent before recording. Session-related diagnostic information may also be processed by the remote-access provider under its privacy practices. Do not display confidential third-party information, financial accounts, health records, or passwords during a session unless necessary for an expressly agreed task. We do not ask customers to disclose passwords over ordinary intake forms or email.

Security events, account access, and backups

We may retain restricted security and access logs to investigate abuse and respond to incidents. Where configured, encrypted backups may be stored with an independent cloud-storage provider to support recovery; deletion from an active account does not necessarily erase a backup immediately. Backup copies are subject to configured retention and recovery practices, legal preservation obligations, and technical constraints. We will respond to security incidents and provide legally required notifications under applicable law.

Requests, retention details, and contact

You may contact support@technicade.tech to ask which personal information we maintain about you, request a copy or correction, ask for deletion, or question processing that affects you. We may need to verify identity and may be unable to delete information that we must keep for legal, security, tax, fraud-prevention, or legitimate dispute purposes. For information controlled by a business workspace customer, we may direct the request to that administrator. We will evaluate the request under applicable law rather than promising every request can be fulfilled.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Technicade products and services evolve. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made. Material changes may also be communicated through the affected product or service when appropriate.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, access, correction, or deletion requests, contact support@technicade.tech.
