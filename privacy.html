Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Technicade collects only the information you choose to provide when you contact us or submit a service request. That information is used to understand your request, respond to you, prepare a quote, schedule work, and provide the requested service.

Information you may provide

  • Your name and email address
  • Details about the device, item, project, or problem
  • Location information you voluntarily provide for an in-person job
  • Photos, files, measurements, or other information you send separately by email

Website forms

Service-request forms on this website are processed using Web3Forms. Form submissions are transmitted through that service so they can be delivered to Technicade by email. Do not submit passwords, financial account credentials, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information through the website form.

How information is used

Submitted information is used for customer communication, quoting, troubleshooting, scheduling, completing requested work, and reasonable business recordkeeping. Technicade does not sell customer contact information.

Remote support

If remote support is needed, you remain in control of the session and approve the connection. Do not share account passwords. In most cases, troubleshooting can be performed without Technicade knowing your passwords.

Third-party services

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and uses Web3Forms for form delivery. Those providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services according to their own policies.

Questions or deletion requests

For privacy questions or to request deletion of information you previously submitted, email technicadegaming@gmail.com.
