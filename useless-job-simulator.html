How to become Senior Vice Potato

Choose a task to earn points and spend energy. Grab coffee when you need a recharge. Every fifth task brings a hilariously urgent management memo—pick a response to continue. Promotions unlock new busywork. Unlock the top title at 1,050 points and keep playing if corporate life calls.

This game uses fictional scores and nonsense job titles. It does not offer real employment, currency, rewards or a leaderboard. Progress is saved only in your browser; use Reset career to clear it.
