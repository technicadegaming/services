Quick questions

Do you really take small jobs?

Yes. Small jobs are part of the point of Technicade. If a visit would cost more than the problem is worth, I will tell you and suggest a cheaper route when I can.

Can I send photos of a broken part?

Yes. Submit a request or email technicadegaming@gmail.com. Clear photos, where the part installs, what it came from, and one known measurement are a great start.

Are the posted prices final?

They are starting prices for the stated scope. Anything requiring additional time, parts, material, shipping or travel is discussed before that extra work begins.

What if my problem is not listed?

Choose “Other / weird problem” and send it anyway. If I can solve it, I will quote it. If not, I will say so.
