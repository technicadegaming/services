Quick questions

What if my job does not match a package?

Choose “Custom Problem” or “Not sure.” The package list is there to make common jobs easy to buy, not to exclude unusual ones.

Can I just send a picture of the broken part?

Yes. A few clear photos, what the part came from, and at least one known measurement are a good starting point. I may ask for the original part so I can measure it accurately.

Do you do really small jobs?

Yes. Small jobs are specifically welcome. If the work is so small that a full visit does not make sense, I will tell you and suggest the cheapest practical route.

Are the prices final?

The listed prices are starting prices for the stated scope. Anything requiring unusual parts, materials, extended labor, multiple prototypes, shipping, or additional travel is quoted before the extra work begins.
