How remote support works

01 / Request

Submit a description and device type through our existing quote form.

02 / Review

We confirm the service, price and appointment before starting.

03 / Approve

If remote access is appropriate, you receive approved session instructions and explicitly authorize access.

04 / Resolve

We troubleshoot together and provide a brief summary of work completed.

Remote access is always opt-in

Technicade does not connect to your device automatically. Remote-control software, if needed, is arranged after a support request and requires your permission. Never share passwords, financial credentials, or one-time authentication codes. You can end a support session at any time.

Full in-browser session launch, customer ticket tracking, and online service checkout are planned features and are not active on this page yet. Current requests use the existing quote form.
