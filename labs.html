How to play

Everything works in your browser, including your phone.

1. Pick your distraction

Try a short reflex game, a silly question, a random challenge, or the forbidden red button.

2. Try to beat yourself

One More Second saves a personal best, while Don't Push the Button tracks reactions and achievements locally.

3. Share with a friend

Copy challenges, share the site, or download an achievement image to send to someone.

Common questions

Are these games actually free?

Yes. The five playable games are free and don't require purchases, accounts, or subscriptions.

Do I need to download anything?

No. They run in a modern web browser on desktop or mobile.

Are scores saved between visits?

Personal bests and button achievements are stored locally in the browser. Clearing website data, changing browsers, or switching devices can reset them.

Does the Challenge Wheel offer prizes?

No. It randomly selects a challenge you can customize. It is a casual activity, not a sweepstakes or gambling game.

Can I suggest another game?

Yes. Use the Technicade contact form to suggest an idea.
