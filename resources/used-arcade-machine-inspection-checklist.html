Buying a Used Arcade Game? What to Inspect Before You Pay

A buyer's checklist for earnings claims, gameplay, electronics, parts availability, freight, and hidden repair costs.

Technicade independent technical resources · Check your equipment manual

Share on Facebook

Quick answer

Request the complete game model and revision, serial information where appropriate, photos of the exact cabinet, a cold-start video, service history, known faults, included accessories, and whether the seller can demonstrate every advertised player station and feature.

Test from a true cold start

Watch the game boot after being fully powered down. Run multiple normal plays on each player position. Test buttons, controllers, bill/card/coin inputs as fitted, speakers, display, lighting, prize delivery, error recovery and operator menus. Ensure a reported failure isn't just hidden by a warm reboot.

Inspect wear and serviceability

Look for cracked plastics, cabinet swelling, water damage, burnt connectors, improvised wiring, failing fans, loose mechanisms, missing locks, damaged harnesses, nonoriginal power supplies, and signs of overheating. Disconnect and inspect internals only with permission and appropriate safety precautions.

Confirm parts, licensing and transfer details

Determine whether critical boards, screens, sensors and proprietary mechanisms remain available. Check whether game software, online services, dongles, accounts, and manufacturer support are transferable. Do not assume an included PC or drive gives you license rights.

Calculate landed and ready-to-operate cost

Purchase price + freight + insurance + loading/unloading + door modifications + installation + necessary repair and parts + expected downtime gives a more realistic total. A cheap machine can become expensive when a proprietary board is unavailable or two technicians are needed to move it.

Verify earnings claims

Treat seller-provided earnings as useful leads, not guarantees. Ask for dated meter photos or verified reports over a meaningful period. Compare to actual play price, location traffic, operating hours, refunds, downtime and nearby competition. Your location may perform very differently.

Protect the transaction

Get a written description of condition and included items, document a successful demonstration, confirm ownership and any liens, and record who is responsible for loading and shipping damage. A purchase should leave enough budget to support the game after arrival.
