⏱️ One More Second

30 seconds. Hit as many targets as you can. Each successful hit moves the target. Can you beat your best?

How to play One More Second

Press Start to begin a 30-second round. Tap or click each target as soon as it appears. Every successful hit adds one point and moves the target to a new location. At zero seconds the round ends automatically. Select Play Again to attempt a higher score.

Quick tip: focus on accuracy before speed, especially on smaller touchscreens. Your best score is saved only in this browser, so you can return and try to beat it later. Different devices keep separate records.
